Implementation Notes

High Priority: These recommendations offer the greatest potential impact and should be implemented first. They typically have shorter payback periods and significant emission reductions.

Medium Priority: Valuable improvements that complement high-priority actions. Consider implementing these as part of a phased approach.

Low Priority: Long-term strategies or supplementary measures to achieve net-zero goals once major emission sources are addressed.

Next Steps: Replace coarse emission factors with official local/regional values, add automated data ingestion, improve forecasting models with additional covariates, and integrate with reporting standards (GHG Protocol, CDP).
